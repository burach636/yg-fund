About us

Yeshiva Mesivta Torah V'Yirah Maharit D'Satmar is a non-profit Torah school (yeshiva) in Monroe, New York. We provide Torah and general education for boys, supported by tuition and by the generous donations of our community.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information we need to run the yeshiva: names, addresses, phone numbers and e-mail addresses of parents, donors and staff, and records of tuition and donations.

We use this information only to communicate with you, to send receipts and statements, and to manage tuition and donations. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us operate (for example, our text-messaging provider), solely to deliver our messages.

To ask about, correct or remove your information, contact us at bt.maharit@gmail.com or (845) 782-1380.

SMS Terms

Program: Yeshiva Mesivta Torah V'Yirah Maharit D'Satmar sends text messages to our own office staff and team members who have agreed to receive them. Staff can text our office number to look up account information, notes and tasks, and receive the answers by text.

How you join: staff members give their mobile number to the yeshiva office and agree to receive these messages. We do not send marketing messages.

Message frequency varies by use. Message and data rates may apply.

To stop receiving messages, reply STOP at any time. You will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. For help, reply HELP or contact us at (845) 782-1380 or bt.maharit@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Privacy Policy above.
